The inlet does the work

At Mach 3 cruise the J58 engine core made only about 17 percent of the thrust. The inlet, with its moving spike, made more than half by slowing and compressing the incoming air. Drag the slider to watch the spike move aft and the shock wave settle on the cowl lip.
