Article 121

This file documents the Lockheed A-12, YF-12, M-21 and SR-71, the aircraft the world came to call the Blackbirds, from the first Archangel studies in 1957 to NASA's last flight in 1999.

Nothing here is new and nothing here is ours. Every photograph, date and figure comes from a public, citable source, and every one is credited and linked. Article 121 was the program's own name for the first A-12 airframe, serial 60-6924.
